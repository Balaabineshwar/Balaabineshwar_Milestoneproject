# Olist E-Commerce Statistical Analysis

Dataset

Table: order_items

Analysis level: Individual order items

Tools: Python, Pandas, and NumPy

# Descriptive Statistics

Mean Price: 120.65

Median Price: 74.99

Mode Price: 59.90

Minimum Price: 0.85

Maximum Price: 6735.00

Variance: 33721.42

Standard Deviation: 183.63

Price and Freight Correlation: 0.4142

# Key Statistical Findings

The mean item price is higher than the median, suggesting that higher-priced items may be pulling the average upward.

The most frequently occurring item price is 59.90.

The large standard deviation indicates substantial variation in item prices.

Price and freight value have a moderate positive linear correlation of approximately 0.4142.

# Business Insights

On Median vs. Mean: The mean is pulled higher by right-skewness, but the median gives us a true picture of the typical price

On Price Spread: Our high standard deviation tells us that Olist has a very diverse product catalog, ranging from low-cost everyday goods to high-end luxury items.

On Price vs. Freight Correlation: While higher-priced items tend to have higher shipping costs (a 0.41 correlation), a high price tag doesn't cause high shipping—it's usually just because those items are heavier or bulkier.
